# Design System Playbook

## Product context
Classify the product and page before selecting a visual style. SaaS consoles, learning platforms, portfolios, retail, hospitality, and editorial sites have different information needs. Establish audience, primary task, trust requirements, content model, and conversion/workflow objective.

## Tokens
Create semantic tokens for color (canvas, surface, raised surface, primary text, secondary text, border, primary action, accent, success, warning, danger, info), type (display, headings, body, small, label, caption, code), spacing, geometry, elevation, motion, and breakpoints. Use OKLCH where useful. Validate contrast in default, hover, disabled, and focus states. Dark mode needs its own surface hierarchy.

## Typography
Use few font families and weights. Give headings a clear scale and intentional line breaks. Keep body copy readable, avoid overly wide text columns, and use tabular numerals for metrics/prices. Do not use monospace, italics, uppercase, or letter spacing as decoration without purpose.

## Composition
Align page edges. Use whitespace to establish grouping. Build a clear focal point and one primary action. Avoid wrapping every section in cards, repeating generic 3-card grids, and filling space with decorative badges. Show the real product or service: accurate UI, relevant photography, diagrams, or real data.

Marketing pages explain what is offered, who it helps, why it is credible, and what action to take. Dashboards prioritize decisions, exceptions, and task completion over decorative KPI tiles.

## Components
Create shared accessible variants for buttons, links, inputs, selects, checkboxes, radios, switches, tabs, dialogs, menus, tooltips, cards, tables, alerts, toasts, navigation, charts, and empty states. Define anatomy, variants, states, spacing, and responsive behavior. Reuse existing project primitives.

## Content and imagery
Use specific, truthful copy and realistic consistent sample data. Never invent testimonials, logos, metrics, prices, certifications, or capabilities. Use relevant real imagery with deliberate crops and reserved dimensions. Use one coherent icon family. Do not use emoji as interface icons.

## Study references, not templates
The supplied reference document points to Apple (motion), Duolingo (onboarding), Linear (UI), Pentagram (typography), Muji (minimalism), Airbnb (search/filter), Aesop (art direction), Notion (dashboards), Stripe (landing-page structure), and Framer (micro-interactions). Extract relevant principles; do not copy surface treatments blindly.
